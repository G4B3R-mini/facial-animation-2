# Pipeline guide

The maintained artist-assisted workflow is [docs/workflow.md](docs/workflow.md):
prepare the model and facial parts, build and visually review the procedural
rig, then animate the accepted rig with a Rhubarb voice test.

See [README.md](README.md) for setup and short commands,
[diagnostics](docs/diagnostics.md) for inspection tools, and
[troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md) for deformation issues.

Canonical fitting and the marker-based wizard are legacy/experimental sources,
not the recommended public workflow.
